The way education works today

We know what was taught.
We know what was tested.
We still don’t always know what was learned.

A teacher spends hours planning, preparing lessons, making assignments and setting papers. A student learns from class, notes, YouTube, tuition and whatever else helps them understand. A school sees marks, attendance and reports.

But these pieces rarely come together.

The result is that teachers work with one piece of the picture, students with another, and schools with another.

  1. Students

    Were spending years and lakhs on education, yet still turning to YouTube when they really wanted to understand something.

  2. Teachers

    Were spending their evenings planning lessons, making slides, setting papers and checking work, doing everything around teaching except teaching.

  3. Schools

    Had more information than ever, yet still struggled to see what was actually happening in their classrooms.

It felt strange.

Everyone was working hard. Everyone had more tools. And yet the pieces still didn’t seem to fit together.

We kept wondering

What if all of this could actually work together?

What if what a teacher taught could shape what a student learned, and what a student learned could help a teacher, and a school, understand what to do next?

Vivran is our attempt to build that.
