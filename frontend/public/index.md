# Vivran (विवरण)

> What if education could finally learn from itself?

Vivran brings together what teachers teach, what students learn, and what classrooms reveal, so every insight deepens understanding, every lesson builds on the last, and education moves closer to what it could be.

Starting with the classroom. Building for all of education.

## What Vivran does today

Vivran is a workspace for teachers and professors, from Class 6 to final year of college. A teacher uploads what they already teach (a syllabus, textbook chapter, lecture, notes or existing papers) and Vivran turns it into the things they need for the classroom, in the way they already teach:

- **Course plans**, week by week, with objectives
- **Lessons and slides**, with speaker notes, exported to PowerPoint
- **Worksheets and activities**, with answer keys
- **Assessments and exam papers**, with marking schemes, set to the teacher's usual format
- **Notes and revision material**
- **Narration, images and short videos**
- **A library** of everything the teacher has made

Each one is built from the teacher's own material, and each one keeps its sources attached.

## Traceable by design

Nothing should appear out of nowhere. When Vivran writes a question, it knows which page, slide or moment in a lecture it came from, so teachers can check it, students can trust it, and schools can stand behind it. A question that no passage in the teacher's material supports is flagged for review, not hidden.

## Who it is for

### Teachers (live today)

For every teacher and professor, from Class 6 to final year. Free, for good: no card, and no trial that runs out.

### Students (in development)

Learning that builds on what the class actually covered:

- Revision built from what the teacher taught
- Practice that points back to the class material, with every mistake explained
- Help with what the student found hard, not everything at once

### Schools and colleges (in development, pilots by conversation)

Everyday teaching, turned into a live view of the whole institution:

- Academic intelligence: understanding, by class and topic
- Early intervention: help, before the test
- Curriculum assurance: taught against plan
- Shared standards: one format per department
- Reporting for boards, accreditation and parents, compiled from the term's work
- One rollout: every teacher, one syllabus, with single sign-on and roles. The institution's data stays its own.

## Where this goes

- **Today:** Vivran helps a teacher prepare a better lesson.
- **Next:** it understands what happened in that lesson, and what to teach again.
- **Eventually:** schools see the same picture. Not just marks at the end of the year, but the story behind them.

## Why we started

Vivran was founded by Vaanchhit Agarwal, a student at SRCC (Shri Ram College of Commerce), New Delhi.

- Students were spending years and lakhs on education, yet still turning to YouTube when they wanted to understand something.
- Teachers were spending their evenings planning lessons, making slides, setting papers and checking work: everything around teaching except teaching.
- Schools had more information than ever, yet still struggled to see what was happening in their classrooms.

Vivran is an attempt to make these pieces work together: what a teacher teaches shapes what a student learns, and what a student learns helps the teacher, and the school, understand what to do next.
